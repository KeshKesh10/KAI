# KAI Conversational Guide

KAI now understands natural language! You don't need to use `/` commands anymore. Here are examples of how to interact naturally:

## 📝 Task Management

Instead of `/task add homework`, just say:
- "Add finish my essay to my tasks"
- "I need to call the dentist"
- "Can you remind me to buy groceries?"
- "Show my tasks"
- "Mark task 1 as done"
- "Clear all my tasks"

## 📚 Study Tools

Instead of `/study save biology photosynthesis`, try:
- "Save a note about photosynthesis"
- "What notes do I have on biology?"
- "Quiz me on chemistry"
- "Show my topics"

## 📅 Calendar Events

Instead of `/calendar add Dentist`, say:
- "Schedule a dentist appointment on Friday"
- "Add a team meeting tomorrow at 2pm"
- "What's my calendar look like?"
- "Remove the dentist appointment"

## 💬 General Conversation

Just chat! KAI understands context:
- "What's the weather going to be like?"
- "Tell me a joke"
- "How do I learn Python?"
- "What's 2+2?"

## ⚡ Still Works: Classic Commands

If you prefer explicit commands, they still work:
- `/task list`
- `/study quiz python`
- `/calendar list`
- `/help`

## 🎯 How It Works

When you type something:
1. KAI looks for keywords related to tasks, studying, or calendar
2. If found, it routes to the appropriate tool
3. If not, it responds conversationally using the local LLM
4. Your conversation history is remembered (last 10 messages)

Just be natural - KAI will understand!
